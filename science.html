The formulas, stated in public.

These are the methods the science library is built to follow. This page does not run them and does not accept farm data. Nothing you read here is uploaded.

Units

Different dimensions are refused. A mass-per-area value can convert to another mass-per-area unit. Temperatures convert through an offset; they are not added as if they were intervals.

Degree days

Averaging method with an optional cap. The result is in °C·day. Inputs are minimum and maximum temperature, a base, and an optional upper cap.

Reference evapotranspiration

FAO-56 Penman–Monteith, Allen et al. 1998, equation 6. Daily reference evapotranspiration in millimetres, from temperature, humidity, wind at 2 m, net radiation, elevation and soil heat flux.

Spectral indices

Reflectance is 0 to 1. NDVI is (NIR − red) / (NIR + red), Rouse et al. 1974. A zero denominator is an error, not a number. The library also carries other published indices; this site does not list proprietary thresholds.

Scientific integrity

  • Observations, estimates, simulations and model interpretations are different kinds of statement. They are not collapsed into one “result”.
  • KutajKutambi output cannot be presented as an observation. A recommendation needs a model, an evaluation, uncertainty and approval.
  • Genomics, transcriptomics and metabolomics records in this build are metadata only. There is no sequence, abundance or enrichment runtime.
  • Computer vision, natural-language assistance and satellite imagery connectors are blocked on systems that are not integrated.
