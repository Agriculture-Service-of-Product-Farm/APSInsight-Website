Home / Business

For a business that depends on farm facts.

A farmer producer organisation, a cooperative, a farm company, or a research group often holds many farms at once. APSInsight is the platform meant to keep each farm’s data from collapsing into one untitled sheet.

Where the help is

An organisation, then a farm

Records are grouped under an organisation and can sit in a project. One member’s field is not silently merged with another’s.

A dataset can be versioned

The catalogue is built so a dataset has versions. Replacing a file is a recorded change, not an invisible overwrite of last season.

A quality note sits with the file

A dataset can carry a quality summary. The point is to notice an empty or broken file before someone uses it for a purchase, a trial, or advice.

Activity is appended

The project keeps an append-only activity record. The public site does not describe how that log is protected, and it does not offer the log itself.

Neighbours, not features of this page

ASPF also works on other agricultural software: a query language for farm data, research on water and soil physics, image-based crop notes, and networking for devices. Those are separate projects. APSInsight is designed so they can connect at a boundary later. This website does not describe their methods, and it does not say they are already running inside APSInsight.

Not part of the claim

  • No marketplace, no payment, and no credit score.
  • No customer list and no savings figure.
  • No statement that a map, a satellite feed, or a sensor network is a finished part of APSInsight.
