Three parts you can point at

apsi-core

Engine

Records, versioned schemas, validation, search, import and export, provenance, quality, retention, legal hold, workflows and a hash-chained audit log.

apsi-science

Science library

Spectral indices, geodesy, statistics, time series, FAO-56 reference evapotranspiration, soil water balance, texture, units and farm economics.

apsi-contracts

ASPF contracts

Versioned ports for AgriQL, KutajKutambi AI, AgriCUDA, APSNetwork and Nityaga. Those products are not built. The adapters report unavailable.

What this build does not pretend

  • PostgreSQL and PostGIS schemas are specified and not executed. Storage is an embedded journal.
  • No object store, Arrow, Parquet, DuckDB or raster I/O.
  • No Excel, database or streaming ingestion. CSV, JSON and NDJSON only.
  • No map UI, durable job queue, ML training, WebSocket channel, OIDC, SAML or MFA. Authentication is static bearer tokens.
  • Satellite and agency connectors are not integrated. Index formulas exist; imagery files do not.
  • AgriQL, KutajKutambi AI, AgriCUDA, APSNetwork and Nityaga RTOS are planned. Only contracts, discovery and a CPU fallback for AgriCUDA ship.
