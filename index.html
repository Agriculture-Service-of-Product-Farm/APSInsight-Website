Agriculture Service of Product Farm

A farm’s measurements, kept as a record.

APSInsight is ASPF’s agricultural data platform. It is built so a farmer or a farm business can keep what was measured, on which field, in which unit, and on which date — and still find that record later.

What it is

APSInsight is not a shop, a loan, or a weather forecast pasted into a chat. It is the place a season’s facts are meant to live: the farm, its fields and plots, the soil sample, and the observation that was actually taken.

For a farmer

The number stays with the field it came from, instead of in a wet notebook or a photo of a sheet.

How that helps

For a business

An organisation can keep each farm’s dataset, see a quality note, and avoid silently replacing last season’s file.

How that helps

What is recorded

Farm, field, plot, soil sample, and an observation with its value, unit and time.

The record

AgriQL and AgriCUDA

APSInsight uses two ASPF languages. AgriQL asks a question of the farm record. AgriCUDA checks a calculation and runs it on the CPU. This website does not run either one.

AgriQL

The agricultural query language. It can read the catalogue, average or count measurements, convert units, and bring a CSV into a table. The question is kept with its result.

AgriCUDA

The agricultural computing language. A program is checked, then run on the CPU. Degree-day totals and a soil-water balance are the kind of work it is for. A GPU and an FPGA are not part of AgriCUDA today.

How the two are supported

What this page will not claim

  • No yield increase, no income figure, and no count of farmers. Those numbers are not facts this site has.
  • No irrigation date, spray dose, or credit decision is produced here.
  • No map product and no live sensor feed are described as finished.
  • AgriQL and AgriCUDA are part of APSInsight. Their source is not printed here. Water and soil research, image-based crop notes, and device networking are separate ASPF projects and are not described on this site.

Where the work stands

Questions

What is APSInsight?

APSInsight is the agricultural data platform of Agriculture Service of Product Farm. It is for keeping a farm’s own measurements — which field, which sample, which number, which unit, and when — in one structured record.

Does this website store my farm data?

No. This website is information only. It has no account, no upload, and no form. Nothing you read here is sent to a server by the page.

Can I sign in and use APSInsight here?

No. The software is in development at ASPF and is not offered for download or sign-in on this page.

Will it tell me when to irrigate or spray?

Not from this website. APSInsight is being built so soil, water and crop observations can be kept with the field they came from. It does not publish a recommendation for your plot here, and it does not promise a yield.

Who is it for?

Farmers who want a durable record, and agriculture businesses such as farmer producer organisations, cooperatives, farm companies and research groups that need the same record to stay traceable.

Does APSInsight use AgriQL and AgriCUDA?

Yes. AgriQL is the query language for farm measurements, including averages and unit conversion. AgriCUDA checks a calculation and runs it on the CPU. This website does not run either. If the AgriQL engine or the AgriCUDA program is not installed, APSInsight does not invent a result. AgriCUDA does not run on a GPU or an FPGA.
