What the record is made of.

These are the facts the APSInsight data model is built to hold. They are described here in ordinary language. Field names, tables, and interfaces are not published on this site.

RecordWhat it is for
OrganisationThe farm business, FPO, cooperative, or research group that owns the work.
FarmThe holding the records belong to.
FieldA named part of that farm.
PlotA smaller piece inside a field, when the work needs that detail.
Soil sampleA sample that can keep its time, depth, and place when those are known.
ObservationOne measurement: what was measured, the value, the unit, when it was taken, and a quality flag.
DatasetA named collection of data, with versions, so a later file does not erase the earlier one without a trace.

Three rules that keep the record honest

  • The unit stays with the value. A depth, a pH, and a weight are not stored as bare numbers that could be misread.
  • The time stays with the observation. A figure from last season is not presented as if it were taken today.
  • A measurement, an estimate, and a suggestion from a model are different kinds of statement. APSInsight is designed not to collapse them into one “result”.

Soil summaries are part of work that is still underway. This page does not calculate a recommendation for any real field.
