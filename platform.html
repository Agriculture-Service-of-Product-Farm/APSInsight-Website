What APSInsight is responsible for.

APSInsight is agriculture-specific data software from Agriculture Service of Product Farm. Its job is to hold datasets, check their quality, and keep farm records in a form that can be audited later. The website you are reading is not that software.

In scope

  • A catalogue of datasets, with versions and a quality note.
  • Farm structure: organisation, farm, field, plot, soil sample, observation.
  • A basic numerical summary of data that has already been recorded.
  • An activity record that is appended, not rewritten in place.
  • A boundary where other ASPF software can be connected later, without copying that software into this explanation.

Not described as finished

  • A public map of fields.
  • A live feed from sensors or satellites.
  • A model registry or an automated recommendation.
  • Sign-in for outside users on this website.

The stage of the work is stated separately, in plain words, on the stage page. That page does not publish source code, configuration, or internal reviews.

Agriculture Service of Product Farm (ASPF) · Azamgarh, Uttar Pradesh

This is an information website. It does not take farm records, and it does not give field advice.
