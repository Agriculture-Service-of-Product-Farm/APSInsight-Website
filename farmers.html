For the person who farms the field.

A season produces more numbers than a person can hold in memory: a soil test, a sowing date, a depth, a litre of water, a count of plants. APSInsight is being built so those numbers stay attached to the field they came from.

The practical help

The field has a name in the record

A farm can hold fields, and a field can hold plots. A later note can point at that same place instead of saying “the plot near the tree”.

A soil sample keeps its circumstances

When a sample is recorded, it can keep the time, the depth, and the place, if those were known when it was taken.

The number keeps its unit

An observation stores the value with the unit and the time. A “6” is not left to mean pH in one book and something else in another.

A bad reading can be marked

The record can carry a quality flag. A doubtful measurement does not have to look the same as a trusted one.

Questions and calculations

An average of soil tests, a unit conversion, or a degree-day total is not done on this website. Inside APSInsight those jobs belong to AgriQL and AgriCUDA. That support is explained separately. It still does not calculate your field from this page.

What a farmer should not expect from this website

  • It will not tell you today’s irrigation or a spray dose.
  • It will not sell seed, fertiliser, or credit.
  • It will not take your plot boundary, your phone number, or a photo of a leaf.
  • A local agronomist, the soil-testing laboratory, and your own eyes remain the people who judge the field. A record only stops the measurement from being lost or mixed up.
